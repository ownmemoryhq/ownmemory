OwnMemory

One memory across all your AIs.

Switch to whichever model is best without starting over. OwnMemory keeps what your assistants learn about you in storage you own, encrypted with a key only you hold.

Open-source release coming soon. Free to run in your own Cloudflare account.

Works where you already chat

Adds memory to the official Claude apps on the web, iPhone and Android through a standard connector. Open-source model apps work too, and more assistants as they open up.

Your storage, your key

Memories are encrypted before they're stored. Your passphrase never leaves your browser, so nobody else can read them, not even whoever runs the server.

Runs in your own account

Deploy your own copy in a few minutes on a free Cloudflare account. No data passes through us, and you can download everything as plain Markdown any time.

How it works

  1. Deploy your own OwnMemory server with one click.
  2. Connect it to Claude and choose a passphrase.
  3. Chat as usual. Claude saves what's worth remembering, and every model you connect can use it.

No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking. Security reports: security@ownmemory.app.
